How to use this table (and its limits):
The table reflects the best available national and state evidence, especially the NIST/NICE brief explicitly stating that “cybersecurity is most often included in the Information Technology Career Cluster,” plus concrete state examples like Texas, Nevada, Missouri, Connecticut, and New Jersey. nist+5

For most states, the primary cluster is IT because that is the dominant pattern across state CTE systems; when state-specific documentation highlighted Business/IT divisions or strong CS standards, those appear in the “secondary” column. nist+3

Because many states implement cybersecurity through both CS standards and IT CTE pathways, the table intentionally shows multiple areas where appropriate rather than forcing a single label. nist+1
